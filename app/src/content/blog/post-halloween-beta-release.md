Happy Halloween! The Lumora team has released the halloween beta release, which is very polished, and it will pave the way for a quicker release!.


## Major Improvements

* Fixed many bugs (interface)
* Calamares failing to launch has been solved.
* WINE and Steam come preinstalled, letting you run your Windows programs right out of the box.
* 3 new wallpapers, and our new default wallpaper chosen

## How to Install?
Download from the top button from the download section, flash it to a USB with atleast 8GB of capacity. Boot the installer (takes about 2 mins), and run the graphical application to install.
